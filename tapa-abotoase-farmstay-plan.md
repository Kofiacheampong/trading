# 🌿 Tapa Abotoase Eco Farmstay — Project Plan

**Vision:** A closed-loop eco cabin + working farm getaway on the Volta Lake, Oti Region. Mud brick architecture, biogas-powered kitchen, farm-to-table meals. Ghana's first proper eco farmstay destination.

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Land title disputes | Medium | Work with chief, get all docs, lawyer |
| Rainy season access | Medium | Check road in wet season, plan drainage |
| Low initial occupancy | Low-Med | Pre-build Instagram audience, start with low rates |
| Construction delays | Medium | Phase builds in dry season (Nov-Mar) |
| Biogas maintenance | Low | Simple digester design, local training |
| Mosquitoes (lakefront) | Medium | Mosquito nets, fans, eco-friendly repellent |
