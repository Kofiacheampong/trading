### 2.2 Market Research
For each selected opportunity:

**Market Size:**
- Research Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Identify Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- Estimate Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

**Competition Analysis:**
- Identify 5-10 key competitors
- Analyze their pricing models
- Identify gaps in the market
- Understand their strengths and weaknesses

**Customer Discovery:**
- Interview 10-15 potential customers
- Validate the problem exists
- Understand willingness to pay
- Identify key features/desired solutions

### 2.3 Financial Modeling
Create basic financial projections:
- Revenue model (subscription, transaction, advertising, etc.)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Break-even analysis
- 3-year financial forecast

## Phase 3: MVP Development (Weeks 5-8)

### 3.1 Prototype Development
Choose the highest-potential opportunity and develop a Minimum Viable Product (MVP):

**For SaaS/Technology:**
- Create a basic version with core features
- Focus on solving the main problem
- Plan for iterative improvements

**For E-commerce:**
- Source initial products
- Set up basic online store
- Test fulfillment process

**For Service Business:**
- Define service offering
- Create service delivery process
- Develop pricing structure

### 3.2 Testing and Feedback
- Test MVP with early adopters
- Collect feedback and usage data
- Iterate based on learnings
- Refine value proposition

## Phase 4: Validation and Scaling (Weeks 9-12)

### 4.1 Validation Metrics
Track key metrics to validate the opportunity:
- Customer acquisition rate
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Revenue growth
- Customer retention rate
- Cost per acquisition vs. lifetime value

### 4.2 Scaling Preparation
If validated, prepare for scaling:
- Operational processes
- Team building requirements
- Funding needs assessment
- Marketing strategy refinement

## Phase 5: Execution Decision (Week 12)

### 5.1 Go/No-Go Decision
Based on results, decide whether to:
- **GO**: Proceed with full business development
- **Pivot**: Adjust the model based on learnings
- **STOP**: Move to the next opportunity on your list

### 5.2 Resource Planning
If proceeding, plan resources needed:
- Funding requirements
- Team building strategy
- Timeline for profitability
- Growth milestones

## Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Financial Risk
- Start part-time while maintaining current income
- Build MVP with minimal investment
- Validate demand before scaling investment
- Maintain emergency funds

### Time Risk
- Start with opportunities requiring minimal time commitment
- Use weekend/evening hours initially
- Gradually increase time investment as validation occurs

### Market Risk
- Thoroughly validate market demand
- Start with problems you personally understand
- Build in flexibility to pivot based on feedback
- Maintain multiple opportunity options

## Success Metrics

### Short-term (3 months):
- Customer interviews completed
- MVP developed and tested
- Initial traction metrics achieved
- Clear path forward identified

### Medium-term (6 months):
- Validated business model
- Repeatable customer acquisition
- Positive unit economics
- Clear scaling plan

### Long-term (12 months):
- Sustainable revenue stream
- Growing customer base
- Clear path to profitability
- Established competitive position
