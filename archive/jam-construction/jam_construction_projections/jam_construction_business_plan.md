# JAM Construction LLC
## Business Plan

### Executive Summary

JAM Construction LLC will launch a specialized construction estimating services business in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Leveraging the commercial construction experience of our lead estimator, we will provide accurate, reliable estimating services to both commercial and residential contractors. Our business model targets the growing demand for professional estimating services in the Twin Cities area, with projected first-year revenue of $987,600 and a clear path to profitability.

### Company Description

JAM Construction LLC is a construction estimating services company specializing in both commercial and residential project estimates. Our unique value proposition lies in our lead estimator's extensive commercial construction experience, which provides deep expertise in complex commercial requirements, regulations, and industry standards while also serving residential contractors.

Our services bridge the gap between large construction firms with in-house estimating capabilities and smaller contractors who need professional estimating services but cannot justify full-time staff. We offer personalized attention with professional results, understanding both project complexity and budget constraints.

### Market Analysis

#### Industry Overview
The construction industry in Minnesota continues to show resilience and growth. The Minneapolis-St. Paul metropolitan area has seen consistent construction activity with commercial construction valued at approximately $2.5 billion annually. Key drivers include:

- Major employers (Target, Best Buy, UnitedHealth Group, 3M) continuing expansion
- Population growth driving residential demand
- Aging housing stock requiring renovation and replacement
- Infrastructure investment and public works projects

#### Target Market
Our primary target market consists of mid-size contractors ($5M-$50M in annual revenue) who need estimating services but are not large enough to maintain full-time estimators. This includes:

1. **Mid-size Commercial Contractors**: Need sophisticated estimating for complex projects but lack in-house expertise
2. **Residential Remodeling Contractors**: Active market in Minneapolis with custom and high-end projects requiring specialized estimating
3. **Government Contractors**: Contractors bidding on state, federal, and local government projects requiring specialized knowledge of public procurement

Secondary markets include small contractors needing occasional estimating support, developers requiring feasibility estimates, and specialty contractors working on government projects.

#### Market Size
The Twin Cities construction market supports numerous contractors who outsource estimating services. With commercial construction valued at $2.5 billion annually and continued residential activity, the market for professional estimating services is substantial and growing.

Additionally, government construction represents a significant opportunity:
- Minnesota Department of Transportation: $2.5 billion annual budget
- State facilities construction: $200-300 million annually
- Hennepin County: $150 million+ annually
- City of Minneapolis: $300 million+ annually
- Federal projects in Minnesota: $100-200 million annually

Government projects often require more detailed estimating due to regulatory compliance, prevailing wage requirements, and extensive documentation - areas where our commercial expertise provides added value.

#### Competitive Landscape
While several large estimating firms operate in the Twin Cities, there is a gap for specialized commercial expertise combined with personalized service. Our competitive advantages include:

- Commercial construction experience
- Dual market capability (commercial and residential)
- Personalized service compared to large firms
- Competitive pricing while maintaining quality
- Local market knowledge
