# Minnesota Unified Certification Program (MNUCP)

## Overview
The Minnesota Unified Certification Program (MNUCP) is the state's certification program for minority, women, and disadvantaged business enterprises (MWDBE). This program provides a single certification that is recognized by the State of Minnesota and participating local agencies, including many metropolitan area counties and cities.

## Program Benefits
- Single certification recognized by multiple agencies
- Access to state and local government contracting opportunities
- Participation in set-aside programs and contract goals
- Networking and business development opportunities
- Technical assistance and mentorship programs

## Certification Categories
1. **Minority Business Enterprise (MBE)**: Businesses at least 51% owned by minority individuals
2. **Women Business Enterprise (WBE)**: Businesses at least 51% owned by women
3. **Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE)**: Businesses owned by socially and economically disadvantaged individuals
4. **Emerging Small Business Enterprise (ESBE)**: Small businesses owned by any ethnicity

## Eligibility Requirements
### Ownership and Control Requirements
- At least 51% ownership by qualifying individuals (for MBE/WBE)
- Social and economic disadvantage (for DBE)
- U.S. citizenship requirement for owners
- Day-to-day management and control by certified owners
- Independence from other firms

### Business Requirements
- Legitimate business concern
- Located in Minnesota or provide substantial benefit to Minnesota
- Adequate financial capacity and business capability
- Personal net worth limitations (excluding business equity and primary residence)

## Application Process
1. **Pre-Application**: Review requirements and gather documentation
2. **Application Submission**: Complete application with required documents
3. **Document Review**: Verification of ownership, control, and business eligibility
4. **Site Visit**: On-site inspection of business operations
5. **Interview**: Potential interview with owners
6. **Committee Review**: Evaluation by certification committee
7. **Notification**: Decision communicated to applicant
8. **Directory Listing**: Inclusion in certified business directory

## Required Documentation
- Completed application form
- Personal financial statements for all owners
- Business financial statements and tax returns
- Organizational documents (articles, operating agreements)
- Banking resolutions and account statements
- Insurance certificates
- Business licenses and permits
- Lease agreements or property ownership documents
- Affidavits of ownership and control
- Narrative statement of business operations

## Certification Benefits for JAM Construction
### Market Access
- State of Minnesota construction and professional services contracts
- Metropolitan area county contracts (Hennepin, Ramsey, etc.)
- Participating city contracts beyond Minneapolis
- Port authority and transit authority projects
- Educational institution contracts

### Competitive Advantages
- Access to MWDBE set-aside contracts
- Contract goal participation opportunities
- Prime contractor subcontracting opportunities
- Joint venture partnerships with larger firms
- Mentor protégé program participation

## Recertification Requirements
- Annual reporting and compliance verification
- Update of financial and business information
- Confirmation of continued eligibility
- Site visit re-verification every 3 years

## Timeline
- Application to certification: 60-90 days
- Recertification: Every 1-3 years depending on circumstances

## Application Strategy for JAM Construction
1. **Determine Appropriate Category**: Select MBE, WBE, DBE, or ESBE based on ownership
2. **Document Preparation**: Gather all required financial and business documents
3. **Application Submission**: Submit complete application to avoid delays
4. **Follow-up**: Respond promptly to any requests for additional information
5. **Marketing Plan**: Develop strategy to reach agencies and prime contractors
6. **Network Building**: Attend MNUCP-sponsored events and workshops

## Related Programs

- MN Department of Transportation DBE Program (federal requirements)
- Local certification programs (may be required in addition to MNUCP)
