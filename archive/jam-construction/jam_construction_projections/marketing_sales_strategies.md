# JAM Construction - Marketing & Sales Strategies

## Sales Process Framework

### Lead Qualification Matrix

**Ideal Customer Profile:**
- Company Size: $2M-$50M annual revenue
- Employee Count: 10-50 employees
- Geographic Focus: Twin Cities metro area
- Services: General construction, specialty trades, residential/remodeling
- Pain Points: Estimating capacity, accuracy, time constraints

**Qualification Questions:**
1. What is your current estimating process?
2. How many projects do you bid monthly?
3. What is your typical project size range?
4. What challenges do you face with current estimating?
5. When do you need the estimate completed?
6. What is your budget for estimating services?
7. Who makes the decision on estimating services?
8. What is your timeline for this project?

### Sales Funnel Optimization

**Stage 1: Lead Generation (Target: 50 leads/month)**
- Marketing activities to generate awareness
- Trade publication advertising
- Networking event attendance
- Referral program implementation
- Digital marketing campaigns

**Stage 2: Lead Qualification (Target: 20 qualified leads/month)**
- Phone qualification calls
- Needs assessment conversations
- Fit evaluation (budget, timeline, requirements)
- Move qualified leads to proposal stage

**Stage 3: Proposal Development (Target: 10 proposals/month)**
- Customized proposals based on client needs
- Value-focused presentations
- Competitive pricing aligned with value
- Follow-up within 48 hours

**Stage 4: Closing (Target: 5 new clients/month)**
- Proposal presentation
- Objection handling
- Contract negotiation
- Onboarding process

**Stage 5: Retention (Target: 85% retention rate)**
- Excellent service delivery
- Regular communication
- Additional service offerings
- Referral requests

## Pricing Strategy

### Service Tier Structure

**Basic Estimate**
- Material takeoffs only
- Simple labor calculations
- Standard project types
- $800-$1,500 per project
- 5-7 business day delivery

**Standard Estimate**
- Complete material and labor
- Equipment and overhead included
- Moderate complexity projects
- $1,200-$2,500 per project
- 7-10 business day delivery

**Premium Estimate**
- Detailed analysis with risk assessment
- Value engineering recommendations
- Multiple scenario modeling
- $2,000-$5,000 per project
- 10-14 business day delivery

### Retainer Programs

**Monthly Retainer Options**
- 10 hours/month: $800/month (effective rate: $80/hour)
- 20 hours/month: $1,500/month (effective rate: $75/hour)
- 30 hours/month: $2,100/month (effective rate: $70/hour)

**Project Retainers**
- Multi-project agreements with volume discounts
- Extended deadline flexibility
- Priority scheduling
- Reduced rates for committed volumes
