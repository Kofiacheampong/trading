# Minnesota Funding Opportunities for JAM Construction

## State-Level Programs

### Minnesota Department of Employment and Economic Development (DEED)

#### Small Business Finance Authority (SBFA) Loan Programs
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $1.5 million
- **Interest Rates**: Typically prime rate + 1-2% (currently around 6-8%)
- **Terms**: Up to 10 years for working capital, up to 25 years for real estate
- **Requirements**: 
  - Minnesota-based business
  - Good credit (typically 650+)
  - Business plan and financial projections
  - Collateral (often 50-80% of loan amount)
- **Application Process**: 4-8 weeks
- **Contact**: (651) 296-3703

#### Microenterprise Loans
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $50,000
- **Interest Rates**: 3-7%
- **Terms**: Up to 7 years
- **Requirements**: 
  - 1-5 employees
  - <$250,000 in annual revenue
  - Personal guarantee
- **Special Features**: May offer technical assistance alongside funding

#### DEED Business Expansion Grant Program
- **Grant Amount**: Up to $100,000 (matching funds required)
- **Eligibility**: Expanding Minnesota businesses creating jobs
- **Requirements**: 
  - Create or retain 1 job per $3,000 of grant
  - Demonstrate economic impact
  - Located in Minnesota

### Minnesota Housing Finance Agency (MHFA)
- **Programs**: Small business loans for community development
- **Focus**: Projects that support housing development
- **Loan Amount**: Varies by program

## Federal Programs Available in Minnesota

### U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) Loans

#### SBA 7(a) Loan Program
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $5 million
- **Guarantee**: Up to 85% for loans under $150,000; 75% for larger loans
- **Interest Rates**: Prime rate + 2.25-4.75% (capped at 14.5%)
- **Requirements**: 
  - For-profit business
  - Demonstrates need for funding
  - Exhausts alternative funding sources
  - Personal guarantee (20%+ ownership)

#### SBA Express Loan Program
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $500,000
- **Processing**: Decision within 36 hours
- **Interest Rates**: Prime + 4.5-6.5%
- **Features**: Streamlined application

#### SBA Microloan Program
- **Administered by**: Nonprofit intermediaries
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $50,000 (typically $13,000 average)
- **Interest Rates**: 8-13%
- **Terms**: Up to 6 years

## Preparing for Funding Applications

### Required Documentation
1. **Business Plan**: Detailed plan including market analysis
2. **Financial Projections**: 3-5 year projections
3. **Personal Financial Statement**: For owners with >20% stake
4. **Tax Returns**: Personal and business (if applicable) for past 2-3 years
5. **Credit Reports**: Personal credit for key principals
6. **Bank Statements**: Recent business and personal
7. **Collateral Documentation**: Property deeds, equipment lists, appraisals
8. **Legal Documents**: Business formation documents, licenses

### Improving Approval Odds
1. **Strong Credit Score**: Aim for 680+ personal credit score
2. **Down Payment**: 10-30% depending on program
3. **Cash Reserves**: 6-12 months of operating expenses
4. **Industry Experience**: Document your girlfriend's commercial construction experience
5. **Market Validation**: Show demand for your services (letters of intent, pilot projects)

## Application Timeline and Process

### Pre-Application (Weeks 1-2)
- Gather required documentation
- Complete business plan and financial projections
- Check credit reports
- Identify potential collateral

### Application Submission (Week 3)
- Submit to primary choice
- Apply to secondary options simultaneously
- Prepare for potential interviews

### Underwriting Process (Weeks 4-8)
- Lender verification of information
- Collateral evaluation
- Credit analysis
- Final approval decision

## Recommended Approach for JAM Construction

### Priority Funding Sequence:

1. **DEED SBFA Microenterprise Loan** (Immediate priority)
   - Matches your estimated funding needs ($8,000)
   - Good interest rates
   - State support for Minnesota businesses

2. **SBA Microloan** (Backup option)
   - Administered through local intermediaries
   - Good for startup businesses
   - Technical assistance often included

3. **Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI)**
   - Mission-driven lenders
   - Relationship-based underwriting
   - May offer more flexible terms

4. **Local Credit Union**
   - Relationship banking approach
   - Potentially more understanding of local market

5. **Online Alternative Lender**
   - As backup for speed
   - Higher cost but faster access

### Application Strategy:
- Start with DEED SBFA as primary target
- Simultaneously research and apply to 1-2 other programs
- Prepare strongest application possible for first submission
- Leverage your girlfriend's commercial experience as a competitive advantage
- Emphasize the market opportunity in the Twin Cities construction market
