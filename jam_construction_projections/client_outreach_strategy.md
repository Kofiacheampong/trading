# JAM Construction - Client Outreach Strategy

## Immediate Outreach Targets (Next 30 Days)

### A. Contractor Database Development
- Compile list of 100+ mid-size contractors ($5M-$50M revenue) in Twin Cities
- Focus on companies with 10-50 employees who likely lack in-house estimator
- Include government contractors with public works experience
- Research contact information for owners/project managers
- Prioritize commercial and government contractors for initial outreach

### B. Direct Contact Campaign
- Week 1: Contact 20 contractors via phone/email
- Week 2: Contact next 20 contractors, follow up on Week 1
- Week 3: Contact final 10 contractors, continue follow-ups
- Week 4: Intensive follow-up campaign on interested prospects

### C. Outreach Script Template
"Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from JAM Construction. We specialize in construction estimating services for contractors in the Twin Cities area. I understand you handle many projects that require accurate estimating. Our lead estimator has extensive commercial experience and can help you win more bids with precise estimates. Could we schedule a brief 15-minute call to discuss how we might support your business? We're offering our first 5 clients a 20% discount on initial estimates."

## Medium-Term Outreach (30-90 Days)

### A. Industry Association Engagement
- Join Associated General Contractors (AGC) of Minnesota
- Attend monthly networking events
- Volunteer for committees to increase visibility
- Sponsor local construction events

### B. Government Contractor Focus
- Join Minnesota Construction Association (focus on public works)
- Attend Minnesota Department of Administration pre-bid conferences
- Participate in MnDOT contractor outreach events
- Connect with government contractors through trade associations
- Attend public project bid openings
- Enroll in City of Minneapolis subcontractor registry (if eligible)
- Pursue DBE/SBE/WBE certifications to access set-aside opportunities
- Market to prime contractors who need certified subcontractors to meet goals

### B. Trade Show Participation
- Research and register for 2-3 construction trade shows
- Prepare booth materials and demonstrations
- Follow up with all trade show contacts within 48 hours

### C. Referral Program Launch
- Create formal referral program with 10% commission
- Approach 20 existing contractors to participate
- Develop referral tracking system
- Recognize top referrers publicly

## Long-Term Relationship Building (90+ Days)

### A. Strategic Partnerships
- Partner with architectural firms for referral relationships
- Connect with lumber yards and supply houses
- Build relationships with insurance adjusters
- Develop connections with real estate developers

### B. Content Marketing
- Publish monthly blog posts on construction trends
- Create quarterly market analysis reports
- Develop case studies (anonymized) showing cost savings
- Offer free educational webinars on estimating best practices

## Digital Marketing Strategy

### A. LinkedIn Campaign
- Optimize company LinkedIn profile for construction keywords
- Share weekly industry insights
- Join 5 construction-related LinkedIn groups
- Direct message 10 contractors per week with personalized messages

### B. Google My Business Optimization
- Claim and optimize Google My Business listing
- Post weekly updates about construction market
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- Encourage satisfied clients to leave reviews

### C. Email Marketing
- Develop monthly newsletter for contractor contacts
- Include market trends, cost updates, and success tips
- Share client testimonials and case studies
- Maintain email list of 200+ construction contacts

## Follow-Up Protocol

### A. Contact Tracking System
- Log all contacts in spreadsheet with dates and outcomes
- Set automated reminders for follow-up calls
- Track conversion rates by outreach method
- Monitor which tactics generate highest ROI

### B. Nurturing Strategy
- Send monthly value-add content to cold prospects
- Offer free 30-minute consultation to warm prospects
- Provide detailed proposals within 48 hours of request
- Follow up within 1 week of proposal delivery

## Success Metrics

### A. Outreach Metrics
- Contacts made per week: Target 20
- Qualified prospects per month: Target 10
- Proposals delivered per month: Target 5
- New clients per month: Target 2

### B. Conversion Tracking
- Percentage of calls resulting in proposals
- Proposal-to-client conversion rate
- Average time from first contact to signed agreement
- Client satisfaction scores

## Resource Requirements

### A. Time Investment
- Owner: 10 hours per week on outreach
- Administrative: 5 hours per week on follow-up
- Total: 15 hours per week initially

### B. Material Needs
