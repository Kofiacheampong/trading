# JAM Construction - Operational Procedures Manual

## Client Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Initial Consultation (Day 0)
- Receive client inquiry via phone, email, or referral
- Schedule 30-minute initial consultation
- Discuss project scope, timeline, and budget requirements
- Explain our process and pricing structure
- Determine if project is a good fit

### Step 2: Project Qualification (Day 1)
- Gather project details: size, type, complexity, timeline
- Assess our capacity and expertise match
- Confirm project falls within our service scope
- Determine preliminary fee based on project size
- Schedule project kickoff meeting

### Step 3: Contract Execution (Day 2)
- Prepare detailed proposal with scope of work
- Include timeline, deliverables, and pricing
- Send contract to client for review and signature
- Receive signed contract and deposit if required
- Enter project into project management system

## Estimating Process Standards

### Phase 1: Document Review (Days 3-5)
- Thoroughly review all plans, specifications, and addenda
- Identify missing or unclear information
- Request additional information from client if needed
- Create initial project outline and checklist
- Estimate time required for complete estimate

### Phase 2: Quantity Takeoff (Days 6-12)
- Perform detailed material takeoffs using digital tools
- Verify measurements and quantities independently
- Cross-reference with historical data for accuracy
- Document all assumptions and exclusions
- Create detailed quantity reports

### Phase 3: Pricing and Assembly (Days 13-16)
- Apply current material costs from supplier quotes
- Factor in labor productivity rates for local market
- Include equipment, permit, and miscellaneous costs
- Apply appropriate markup for overhead and profit
- Prepare detailed cost breakdown by trade

### Phase 4: Quality Assurance (Days 17-18)
- Independent review by senior estimator
- Verification of calculations and assumptions
- Check against similar historical projects
- Review with client for clarification if needed
- Finalize estimate with supporting documentation

### Phase 5: Delivery (Day 19)
- Prepare professional estimate report
- Include executive summary and detailed breakdowns
- Provide digital and printed copies as requested
- Conduct delivery meeting to explain estimate
- Provide post-delivery support as agreed

## Quality Control Standards

### Accuracy Requirements
- All measurements verified twice
- Quantities cross-checked with plan dimensions
- Unit prices validated with current market rates
- Total calculations verified independently
- Final review by senior team member

### Documentation Standards
- All assumptions clearly stated
- Exclusions specifically identified
- Source of unit prices documented
- Calculations shown for verification
- Project conditions noted and priced appropriately

### Review Process
- Peer review of all estimates >$50,000
- Senior review of all estimates >$100,000
- Random sampling review of smaller estimates
- Client feedback incorporated into process improvement
- Continuous training based on review findings
