# Government Contracting Research for JAM Construction

## Minnesota Government Construction Opportunities

### State Level Opportunities

#### Minnesota Department of Transportation (MnDOT)
- **Annual Construction Budget**: Approximately $2.5 billion
- **Project Types**: Highway construction, bridge projects, road improvements
- **Opportunities for Estimating**: 
  - Pre-bid estimating for contractors bidding on MnDOT projects
  - Change order analysis during project execution
  - Material takeoffs for specialized work
- **Bid Schedule**: Monthly letting of projects
- **Requirements**: Certified Payroll (prevailing wage), DBE participation goals

#### Minnesota Department of Administration
- **Annual Construction Budget**: $200-300 million for state facilities
- **Project Types**: State office buildings, universities, hospitals, correctional facilities
- **Opportunities**: Large-scale commercial estimating expertise highly valued

#### Minnesota Pollution Control Agency
- **Project Types**: Environmental infrastructure, water treatment facilities
- **Opportunities**: Specialized construction estimating

### Federal Opportunities in Minnesota

#### GSA (General Services Administration)
- **Project Types**: Federal buildings, courthouses, post offices
- **SBA Small Business Goals**: 23% of federal prime contracting to small businesses
- **Certifications Helpful**: 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOB, WOSB

#### U.S. Army Corps of Engineers
- **Project Types**: Flood control, environmental restoration, water resource projects
- **Location**: St. Paul District Office serves Minnesota/Wisconsin
- **Annual Projects**: $100-200 million in construction

#### USDA/Rural Development
- **Project Types**: Rural infrastructure, water and waste disposal
- **Geographic Focus**: Greater Minnesota outside metro area

### Local Government Opportunities

#### Ramsey County
- **Annual Construction Budget**: $100 million+

#### City of Saint Paul

- **Project Types**: Public facilities, streets, parks, utilities

### Government Contracting Requirements

#### Bonding Requirements
- **Performance Bonds**: Typically 100% of contract value
- **Payment Bonds**: Typically 100% of contract value
- **Bid Bonds**: Usually 5-10% of bid amount
- **Surety Companies**: Must be Treasury-certified

#### Insurance Requirements
- **General Liability**: $1-2 million minimum
- **Workers' Compensation**: Required by law
- **Professional Liability**: Highly recommended for estimators

#### Certification Programs
- **Minority Business Enterprise (MBE)**: Minnesota Unified Certification Program
- **Women Business Enterprise (WBE)**: State certification available
- **Small Business Enterprise (SBE)**: State program
- **Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE)**: Federal certification

### Prevailing Wage Requirements

#### Minnesota Prevailing Wage Law
- Applies to public works projects over $1,000
- Wage rates determined by Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry
- Penalties for non-compliance
- Monthly reporting requirements

#### Federal Davis-Bacon Act
- Applies to federal construction projects
- Wage determinations by U.S. Department of Labor
- Certified payroll requirements

### Government Estimating Specifics

#### Different Requirements from Private Projects
- **More Detailed Documentation**: Government projects require extensive documentation
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Multiple layers of oversight and compliance
- **Change Order Procedures**: Strict protocols for modifications
- **Timeline Adherence**: Penalties for delays in government projects

#### Estimating Considerations for Government Projects
- **Prevailing Wage Calculations**: Factor in higher wage rates
- **Productivity Adjustments**: Government projects may have additional requirements
- **Extended Overhead**: Longer project durations may affect overhead calculations
- **Compliance Costs**: Factor in additional compliance requirements

### Minnesota Public Procurement Process

#### Pre-Bid Phase
- **Plan Rooms**: Plans available for review before bidding
- **Pre-Bid Meetings**: Required attendance for many projects
- **Site Visits**: Often mandatory for major projects
- **Bid Protests**: Process for challenging bid specifications

#### Bidding Process
- **Sealed Bids**: Most public projects use sealed bid process
- **Public Opening**: Bids opened publicly at specified time
- **Low Responsive and Responsible Bidder**: Award to lowest qualified bidder
- **Debarment List**: Check for debarred contractors

#### Post-Award Process
- **Contract Execution**: Quick turnaround required after award
- **Performance Security**: Bonds required before contract execution
- **Progress Payments**: Monthly billing with retention
- **Final Payment**: After completion and acceptance

### Opportunities for Estimating Services in Government Sector

#### Pre-Bid Services
- **Detailed Estimating**: For contractors bidding on public projects
- **Risk Assessment**: Analysis of government contract requirements
- **Compliance Review**: Ensuring bids meet all requirements
- **Bonding Support**: Helping contractors understand bonding requirements

#### Post-Award Services
- **Cost Tracking**: Monthly cost reporting and analysis
- **Change Order Pricing**: Government change order procedures
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Ensuring adherence to contract terms
- **Final Cost Analysis**: Project closeout and analysis

### Marketing to Government Contractors

#### Target Market
- **Established Government Contractors**: Companies with existing government experience
- **Growing Contractors**: Companies looking to expand into government market
- **Specialty Contractors**: Those focusing on specific government project types

#### Marketing Approach
- **Certification Assistance**: Help clients navigate certification processes
- **Education**: Seminars on government contracting requirements
- **Network Building**: Relationships with established government contractors

### Funding and Financing for Government Contractors

#### Government Contractor Financing
- **Contract Financing**: Lenders familiar with government payment cycles
- **SBA Surety Bond Guarantee**: For contractors needing bonding assistance
- **Equipment Financing**: For specialized government construction equipment

#### Payment Considerations
- **Payment Delays**: Government payments often slower than private sector
- **Retention**: Standard 10% retention on government projects
- **Prompt Payment**: Minnesota law requires payment within 30 days
