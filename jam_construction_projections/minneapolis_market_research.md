# Minneapolis Construction Market Research for JAM Construction

## Market Overview

### Construction Activity in Minneapolis
- Minneapolis-St. Paul metro area has seen consistent construction growth
- Commercial construction valued at approximately $2.5 billion annually
- Residential construction remains strong with ongoing housing demand
- Major employers driving commercial demand: Target, Best Buy, UnitedHealth Group, 3M

### Estimating Services Market
- High demand for accurate estimating services due to increased construction costs
- Commercial projects typically require more complex estimating (your girlfriend's specialty)
- Average commercial project sizes range from $500K to $50M+
- Residential projects typically range from $100K to $2M+

### Key Market Trends (2025)
1. **Labor Shortage**: Skilled labor shortage continues to drive need for efficient estimating
2. **Material Cost Volatility**: Requires precise estimating to maintain margins
3. **Technology Adoption**: Increasing demand for digital estimating tools
4. **Sustainability**: Growing demand for green building practices and LEED knowledge

## Target Customer Segments

### Primary Targets
1. **Mid-size Commercial Contractors** ($5M-$50M in annual revenue)
   - Need estimating services but not large enough to maintain full-time estimator
   - Projects typically $500K-$10M
   - Willing to pay premium for accurate estimates

2. **Residential Remodeling Contractors**
   - Active market in Minneapolis due to aging housing stock
   - Projects typically $50K-$500K
   - Often need specialized estimating for custom work

### Secondary Targets
1. **Subcontractors** needing bid assistance
2. **Developers** requiring feasibility estimates
3. **Insurance companies** for property damage assessments

## Competitive Landscape
- Several large estimating firms in the Twin Cities
- Many small contractors still do estimating in-house
- Opportunity for specialized commercial estimator with proven experience
- Limited competition for contractors specializing in both commercial and residential

## Pricing Strategy
- Commercial estimates: $1,000-$5,000 per project (based on size)
- Residential estimates: $500-$2,000 per project
- Retainer arrangements for high-volume clients
- Hourly rates: $75-$125/hour for complex projects

## Geographic Coverage
- Primary focus: Minneapolis-St. Paul 7-county metro area
- Secondary: Greater Minnesota and surrounding Wisconsin
- Radius: Up to 100 miles from Minneapolis

## Seasonal Considerations
- Winter slowdown affects construction start dates (Dec-Feb)
- Estimating work can continue year-round indoors
- Spring/Summer peak seasons for new construction starts
- Fall typically busy for next-year planning projects
