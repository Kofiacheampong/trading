## 🐔 PROMPT 1: POULTRY FARM BRAND IDENTITY

```
You are a professional brand identity designer with 15+ years of experience creating brands for agricultural businesses in Africa.

PROJECT BRIEF:
I need a complete brand identity for a commercial poultry farm in Ghana.

BUSINESS DETAILS:
- Business Type: Commercial Poultry Farm (Broiler Chickens)
- Location: Rural Ghana (3-acre farm)
- Scale: Starting 2,000 birds, scaling to 4,000+ within 6 months
- Target Market: Local markets, restaurants, hotels, institutions, direct consumers
- Products: Live broiler chickens (2.5-3.5 kg, 8-week growth cycle)
- Pricing: GHS 95-110 per bird (market competitive)
- Unique Value: Fresh (not frozen), locally-produced, reliable supply, quality assurance

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Primary: Market women, local retailers (40% of sales)
- Secondary: Restaurant/hotel owners (30% of sales)
- Tertiary: Schools, churches, institutions (20% of sales)
- Direct: Individual consumers, families (10% of sales)
- Demographics: Ghanaian, all ages, value fresh quality protein
- Psychographics: Prefer local over imported, support community businesses, quality-conscious

BRAND PERSONALITY:
- Trustworthy and reliable
- Quality-focused
- Community-oriented
- Professional but approachable
- Modern farming practices
- Ghanaian pride (local production)

DELIVERABLES NEEDED:

1. BUSINESS NAME OPTIONS (10 suggestions)
   - Should reflect: quality, freshness, local, trust, growth
   - Easy to pronounce in Ghana (English + local languages)
   - Memorable and distinctive
   - Check for cultural appropriateness
   - Include meaning/rationale for each

2. LOGO CONCEPTS (describe 5 concepts in detail)
   - Should work on: signage, business cards, vehicle decals, uniforms, packaging
   - Include: icon/symbol + wordmark
   - Style: Modern, clean, professional
   - Colors: Suggest palette with rationale
   - Should look good in color and black/white
   - Describe each concept visually (I will give to designer)

3. COLOR PALETTE
   - Primary colors (2-3)
   - Secondary colors (2-3)
   - Accent colors (1-2)
   - Include hex codes
   - Explain psychology/rationale for each

4. TYPOGRAPHY
   - Primary font (for logo/headlines)
   - Secondary font (for body text)
   - Should be available in Google Fonts or commonly available
   - Explain rationale

5. BRAND VOICE & TONE
   - How we communicate (adjectives)
   - Language style (formal/casual)
   - Key messages to convey
   - Words/phrases to use
   - Words/phrases to avoid

6. TAGLINE OPTIONS (10 suggestions)
   - Short, memorable (3-7 words)
   - Communicates value proposition
   - Works in marketing materials

7. BRAND APPLICATIONS
   - How brand appears on: business cards, signage, vehicle decals, staff uniforms, 
     delivery bags, invoices, social media, website
   - Describe for each application

8. COMPETITOR DIFFERENTIATION
   - How this brand stands out from: imported frozen chicken, other local farms, 
     backyard poultry sellers
   - Visual and messaging differentiation

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS:
- Must resonate with Ghanaian market (urban and rural)
- Should work across cultures and languages in Ghana
- Avoid anything that could be culturally insensitive
- Should scale (work for small farm and large operation)
- Consider practical application (signage in rural areas, print quality, etc.)
- Budget-conscious (design should be producible locally)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Present in a structured brand guidelines document format with clear sections.
Include visual descriptions that a graphic designer can execute.
Provide rationale for all recommendations.

Make it comprehensive but actionable.
```

## 💻 PROMPT 2: COMPUTER LITERACY FOUNDATION BRAND IDENTITY

```
You are a professional brand identity designer with expertise in non-profit and education sector branding in Africa.

PROJECT BRIEF:
I need a complete brand identity for a new non-profit foundation focused on digital literacy in rural Ghana.

ORGANIZATION DETAILS:
- Organization Type: Non-Profit Foundation (Company Limited by Guarantee)
- Mission: Bridge the digital divide in rural Ghana through accessible computer literacy training
- Vision: A Ghana where every rural community member has digital skills to thrive in the 21st century
- Programs: Computer training centers in rural communities (3 centers in Year 1)
- Target: 500-1,000 students trained in Year 1
- Focus Areas: Basic computer literacy, productivity software, digital skills for employment, 
  specialized tracks (entrepreneurship, tech skills, creative skills)
- Beneficiaries: Youth (40%), Students (25%), Adults (25%), Seniors (10%)
- Special Focus: 60% women and girls, 10% persons with disabilities
- Geographic Focus: Rural Ghana (starting with 3-5 communities)

TARGET AUDIENCE:
Primary Audiences:
- Rural youth (15-29 years) seeking employment skills
- Parents of school-age children
- Community leaders (chiefs, assembly members, religious leaders)

Secondary Audiences:
- Grant makers and donors (Mastercard Foundation, MTN Foundation, GNPC, etc.)
- Corporate partners (technology companies, telecoms)
- Government agencies (Ministry of Education, Ministry of Communications)
- Media and general public

BRAND PERSONALITY:
- Empowering and transformative
- Trustworthy and credible
- Accessible and inclusive
- Modern and forward-thinking
- Community-centered
- Professional and accountable
- Hopeful and optimistic

BRAND VALUES:
- Accessibility (training for all, regardless of economic status)
- Quality (high-standard curriculum and instruction)
- Practicality (skills directly applicable to jobs and life)
- Community (programs designed with and for local communities)
- Inclusion (special focus on women, girls, persons with disabilities)
- Sustainability (long-term impact through sustainable operations)

DELIVERABLES NEEDED:

1. ORGANIZATION NAME OPTIONS (10 suggestions)
   - Should reflect: digital, literacy, empowerment, opportunity, Ghana, future, skills
   - Easy to pronounce and remember
   - Work in English and be adaptable to local languages
   - Available as .org domain (check availability if possible)
   - Not already in use by other organizations in Ghana
   - Include meaning/rationale for each
   - Consider acronym potential (should be positive/meaningful)

2. LOGO CONCEPTS (describe 5 concepts in detail)
   - Should work on: website, letterheads, banners, training materials, certificates, 
     social media, merchandise, signage
   - Include: icon/symbol + wordmark options
   - Style: Modern, clean, inspiring, professional
   - Should convey: technology, education, empowerment, Ghana/Africa
   - Colors: Suggest palette with rationale
   - Should work in color, black/white, and single color
   - Describe each concept visually (for designer execution)
   - Avoid clichés (no generic globes, handshakes, etc.)

3. COLOR PALETTE
   - Primary colors (2-3)
   - Secondary colors (2-3)
   - Accent colors (1-2)
   - Include hex codes, RGB, and CMYK
   - Explain psychology/rationale for each
   - Consider cultural meanings in Ghana/Africa
   - Ensure accessibility (color blindness friendly)

4. TYPOGRAPHY
   - Primary font (for logo/headlines)
   - Secondary font (for body text)
   - Web-safe fonts preferred
   - Should support special characters if needed
   - Explain rationale for each

5. BRAND VOICE & TONE
   - How we communicate (adjectives)
   - Language style (aspirational yet grounded)
   - Key messages to convey
   - Words/phrases to use (empowering, inclusive language)
   - Words/phrases to avoid (patronizing, tech jargon)
   - Tone variations for different audiences (donors vs. beneficiaries)

6. TAGLINE OPTIONS (10 suggestions)
   - Short, memorable (3-8 words)
   - Communicates mission and impact
   - Inspiring and actionable
   - Works across cultures
   - Include rationale for each

7. BRAND APPLICATIONS
   - Describe brand appearance on: website, letterheads, business cards, training 
     certificates, student ID cards, banners, roll-up displays, training manuals, 
     pens/notebooks, t-shirts, social media graphics, email signatures, 
     grant proposals, annual reports
   - Include do's and don'ts for each

8. IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
   - Types of photos to use
   - Photography style (candid vs. posed, lighting, composition)
   - How to depict beneficiaries (dignified, empowered vs. pitiful)
   - Stock photo guidance
   - Iconography style

9. DONOR/GRANTMAKER CONSIDERATIONS
   - How brand appeals to institutional funders
   - Professionalism for grant applications
   - Impact visualization
   - Reporting template branding

10. COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT
    - How brand resonates with rural communities
    - Cultural sensitivity considerations
    - Local language adaptations
    - Community ownership of brand

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS:
- Must work across Ghana's diverse cultures and languages
- Should appeal to both rural beneficiaries and urban/international donors
- Avoid poverty porn or patronizing imagery
- Represent dignity and empowerment, not pity
- Should scale (from 1 center to 25+ centers)
- Consider practical application in rural areas (print quality, color availability)
- Digital-first but also works in print
- Budget-conscious (design should be producible locally)
- Should attract corporate volunteers and partners

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Present in a comprehensive brand guidelines document format with clear sections.
Include visual descriptions that a graphic designer can execute.
Provide rationale for all recommendations.
Make it inspiring yet practical and actionable.
```

## 🐔 PROMPT 3: POULTRY FARM LANDING PAGE

```
You are a professional web designer and copywriter specializing in agricultural business websites in Africa.

PROJECT BRIEF:
Create a complete landing page for a commercial poultry farm in Ghana.

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
- Commercial poultry farm (broiler chickens)
- Location: Rural Ghana (3-acre farm)
- Scale: 2,000 → 4,000+ birds within 6 months
- Products: Live broiler chickens (2.5-3.5 kg)
- Price: GHS 95-110 per bird
- Target: Local markets, restaurants, hotels, institutions, consumers
- Unique Value: Fresh (not frozen), local, reliable supply, quality

WEBSITE GOALS:
1. Generate leads from potential buyers (restaurants, hotels, institutions)
2. Provide information for individual customers
3. Build credibility and trust
4. Showcase farm operations and quality standards
5. Enable easy contact/ordering
6. Improve with SEO for local search

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Restaurant/hotel owners looking for reliable chicken supply
- Institution purchasers (schools, churches, companies)
- Market women and retailers
- Individual consumers (families, event planners)
- Potential partners and investors

LANDING PAGE STRUCTURE:

Create a complete single-page website with these sections:

1. HEADER/NAVIGATION
   - Logo placement
   - Navigation menu items
   - Contact CTA button
   - Mobile-responsive design notes

2. HERO SECTION
   - Compelling headline (5 options)
   - Subheadline
   - Hero image description (what photo to use)
   - Primary CTA button
   - Trust indicators (if any)

3. ABOUT US SECTION
   - Brief farm story
   - Mission/values
   - Founder message (optional)
   - Farm photo description
   - Key facts (acreage, capacity, etc.)

4. PRODUCTS SECTION
   - Product offerings (live birds, bulk orders, etc.)
   - Specifications (weights, ages)
   - Pricing information (or "contact for pricing")
   - Product photos description
   - Order minimums

5. WHY CHOOSE US SECTION
   - 4-6 key differentiators
   - Icons + short descriptions
   - Compare to alternatives (frozen, other farms)
   - Quality assurance points

6. HOW IT WORKS SECTION
   - Simple 3-4 step process
   - From order to delivery
   - Timeline expectations
   - Visual flow description

7. CUSTOMERS/TESTIMONIALS SECTION
   - Customer testimonial placeholders (3-4)
   - Customer types we serve
   - Logos of customer types (icons)
   - Success metrics (if applicable)

8. GALLERY SECTION
   - Photo grid description (6-8 photos)
   - Types of photos to include
   - Caption suggestions

9. FAQ SECTION
   - 6-8 common questions with answers
   - Cover: ordering, delivery, pricing, quality, storage, etc.

10. CONTACT/ORDER SECTION
    - Contact form fields
    - Phone numbers
    - Email
    - Physical address/location
    - Map integration notes
    - Business hours
    - Social media links

11. FOOTER
    - Quick links
    - Contact info
    - Social media
    - Copyright
    - Any certifications/logos

COPYWRITING REQUIREMENTS:
- Tone: Professional, trustworthy, friendly, Ghanaian context
- Language: Clear, simple English (accessible to all)
- Length: Concise but informative
- CTAs: Clear, action-oriented
- SEO: Include relevant keywords naturally

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Fast loading (optimize for Ghana internet speeds)
- WhatsApp integration for quick contact
- Click-to-call phone numbers
- Simple, clean design
- Easy to update/maintain
- Hosting recommendations for Ghana

DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS:
- Color scheme (based on brand identity)
- Typography recommendations
- Image style and requirements
- Icon style
- Button styles
- Spacing and layout guidelines

CONTENT NEEDED FROM US:
List what content/photos we need to provide to build this.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide complete wireframe description with:
- Section-by-section layout
- Actual copy for all sections (not just placeholders)
- Design notes for each section
- Mobile vs. desktop considerations
- Technical implementation notes
- Recommended platform (WordPress, Webflow, etc.)

Make it ready to hand to a web developer or build with AI website builder.
```

13. CONTACT SECTION

Prioritize donor appeal while remaining authentic to mission.

## 📋 HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

### For Brand Identity:
1. **Copy Prompt 1 or 2** (depending on project)
2. **Fill in any blanks** (like specific preferences)
3. **Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or similar AI**
4. **Review output** and refine with follow-up questions
5. **Take concepts to graphic designer** or use AI image generators

### For Landing Pages:
1. **Copy Prompt 3 or 4** (depending on project)
2. **Review and customize** any sections
3. **Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or similar AI**
4. **Get complete wireframe + copy**
5. **Use with:**
   - AI website builders (Framer AI, Wix ADI, etc.)
   - Web developers (as detailed brief)
   - WordPress page builders (Elementor, etc.)

### For Logo Generation:
1. **Wait until you have name and colors** from brand identity work
2. **Copy Prompt 5** and fill in name/colors
3. **Use in Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or similar**
4. **Take best concepts to designer** for refinement

### Recommended AI Tools:

**Brand Identity:**
- ChatGPT-4 or Claude-3 (for strategy and concepts)
- Midjourney or DALL-E 3 (for visual concepts)

**Landing Pages:**
- ChatGPT-4 or Claude-3 (for copy and structure)
- Framer AI, Wix ADI, or Durable (for AI website building)
- WordPress + Elementor AI (for more control)

**Logos:**
- Midjourney v6 (best for logo concepts)
- DALL-E 3 (good for initial ideas)
- Looka or Brandmark (AI logo generators)
