# Tapa Abotoase — Land Scouting Brief

## Target Area
Lakefront or lake-view plots near Tapa Abotoase, Biakoye District, Oti Region. Look for spots between the town and the Volta Lake shoreline.

---

## For the Relative on the Ground — Forward This

**Hey, can you help me check out some land near Tapa Abotoase for a project? Here's what I need:**

📍 **Location Scout**
1. Walk towards the lake from Tapa Abotoase. Find open/unused plots along the way, especially ones with a view of the water
2. Take a video — slowly spin 360° so I can see everything
3. Take photos at different times: morning (~8am), noon, and late afternoon (golden hour light)

🚗 **Access**
4. How do you get there from the main road? Is the road tarred, gravel, or dirt?
5. In the rainy season — does it get muddy/bad? Can a regular car make it?

💧 **Water**
6. Is there a stream, river, or direct lake access on any plot?
7. Ask locals about boreholes — how deep, water quality

👥 **Who to Talk To**
8. Ask around: "Who owns the land between here and the lake?" Start with the town chief or assemblyman
9. Is any of it for sale? What's the price per acre roughly?
10. Any disputes over the land? (Important — check this)

🌿 **The Land Itself**
11. Is it bushy, farmed, or empty? Take pictures of the soil (is it reddish clay?)
12. Any big trees that would be good for shade/natural cooling?
13. Is it flat enough to build on? Any slope?

🍌 **Farming**
14. What grows well right there? What do neighbors farm?
15. Is the soil good for vegetables, cassava, plantain, cocoa?

---

## What I'm Looking For (for your reference)

**Ideal plot:**
- Lake view or lake-adjacent
- Decent road access (passable year-round)
- At least 2-5 acres
- Good reddish clay soil (for mud brick building)
- Available for purchase without ownership drama
- Not too close to town noise but close enough to walk to the barter market

**Budget guess:** Need to know local land prices per acre first
