## 11. REFERENCE CHECK SCRIPT

### Phone Call Script for References

**Introduction:**
"Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I'm evaluating [Contractor Name] for a poultry farm construction project. They listed you as a reference. Do you have 5-10 minutes to answer a few questions about your experience working with them?"

**Questions:**

1. **Project Overview:**
   "What project did [Contractor] complete for you?"
   "When was the project completed?"
   "What was the approximate value?"

2. **Timeline:**
   "Was the project completed on time?"
   "If there were delays, what caused them?"
   "How did the contractor handle delays?"

3. **Budget:**
   "Was the project completed within budget?"
   "Were there many change orders?"
   "How were cost overruns handled?"

4. **Quality:**
   "How would you rate the quality of work? (1-10)"
   "Have there been any issues since completion?"
   "How did the contractor handle warranty/punch list items?"

5. **Communication:**
   "Was the contractor responsive and easy to communicate with?"
   "Did they provide regular progress updates?"

6. **Overall:**
   "Would you hire this contractor again?"
   "Would you recommend them to others?"
   "Any other comments or warnings I should know about?"

**Closing:**
"Thank you very much for your time. This information is very helpful for my evaluation."

## 12. SITE VISIT CHECKLIST

### When Visiting Contractor's Completed Projects

**Exterior:**
- [ ] Roof condition (no leaks, proper drainage)
- [ ] Wall condition (no cracks, proper finish)
- [ ] Foundation (no settling, cracks)
- [ ] Doors and windows (proper fit, functioning)
- [ ] Paint/finish quality

**Interior:**
- [ ] Floor quality (level, no cracks)
- [ ] Wall finish (smooth, even)
- [ ] Ceiling (level, no water stains)
- [ ] Electrical (proper installation, functioning)
- [ ] Plumbing (no leaks, proper drainage)

**Structural:**
- [ ] Overall alignment
- [ ] Door/window operation
- [ ] Evidence of settling
- [ ] Ventilation adequacy

**Ask Building Owner:**
- How long since completion?
- Any issues experienced?
- How responsive was contractor for warranty work?
