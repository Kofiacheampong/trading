### 3.6 Biosecurity and Health Management

**Biosecurity Protocols:**
- Restricted farm access (authorized personnel only)
- Foot baths at all entry points
- Vehicle disinfection
- No visitors without approval
- Quarantine area for new/sick birds
- Regular disinfection of facilities
- Pest and rodent control program
- Proper waste disposal

**Vaccination Schedule:**

| Age | Vaccine | Disease | Method |
|-----|---------|---------|--------|
| Day 1 | Marek's | Marek's Disease | Injection (at hatchery) |
| Day 7 | Lasota | Newcastle Disease | Eye drop / Drinking water |
| Day 14 | Gumboro | Infectious Bursal Disease | Drinking water |
| Day 21 | Lasota | Newcastle Disease (booster) | Eye drop / Drinking water |
| Day 28 | Gumboro | Gumboro (booster) | Drinking water |
| Week 6-8 | Fowl Pox | Fowl Pox | Wing web (if endemic) |

**Common Diseases and Prevention:**

| Disease | Symptoms | Prevention | Treatment |
|---------|----------|------------|-----------|
| Newcastle | Respiratory, nervous signs | Vaccination | Supportive care |
| Gumboro | Diarrhea, depression | Vaccination | Supportive care |
| Coccidiosis | Bloody droppings | Clean litter, medication | Anticoccidials |
| Fowl Pox | Lesions on skin | Vaccination | Supportive care |
| CRD | Respiratory signs | Good ventilation | Antibiotics |
