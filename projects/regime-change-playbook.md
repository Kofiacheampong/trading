## 1. The Signals (leading indicators — watch these, in order)

| Signal | Warning Level | Meaning |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **VIX level** | > 20 = caution, > 25 = red zone | Fear is being priced |
| **VIX rate of change** | +20% in one day, or 14 → 25+ in a week | THE tell — fast spikes are the warning, not the level |
| **VIX term structure** | Front-month futures > 6-month (backwardation) | Market pricing near-term crisis |
| **Breadth** | % of S&P stocks above 50-day MA < 40% | Rally narrowing = tired tape |

| **The leaders** | NVDA / NBIS / CRWV / SMCI break 50-day MA hard | AI-infra mania unwinding = this cycle's canary |
| **New lows** | NYSE 52-wk lows expanding while VIX still low | Quiet deterioration before the spike |

**Rule: 3+ signals firing = regime change likely. 1 signal = noise. Don't trade on one.**

## 2. The Responses (pre-committed — decide now, not in panic)

### If VIX crosses 20 (caution zone):
- **Momentum scanner stops automatically** (needs VIX < 20) — let it. No manual override.
- **Weekly options: reduce to $100 floor risk, 1 trade max/week** (from $100-150, 1-2/wk)
- **No new call flags, no new put fades** until VIX < 20 again
- Check the sim's put win rate — it'll show the edge thinning before real money does

### If VIX crosses 25 (red zone):
- **All new weekly options OFF.** Dry powder only.
- **MES sims go flat-heavy** — the OR-fade strategy needs chop; trends kill it. Watch it, don't trade it.
- **Cash target: raise to 50%+ of stock portfolio**
- If holding a US index position: this is when the cheap puts (bought at VIX 14) finally pay

### If VIX spikes fast (14 → 25 in < 1 week):
- **Do NOT buy the dip in the first 48 hours.** Fast VIX spikes mean the bottom isn't in.
- Wait for the first *lower* VIX close (VIX falls while market falls = capitulation) — that's the entry window.
- That's the moment to deploy the dry powder, in tranches, not all at once.
