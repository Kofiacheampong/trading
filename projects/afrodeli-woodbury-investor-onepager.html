Term Sheet & Exit (Appendix G / Section 9.15)

  • Vehicle: Minnesota manager-managed LLC; manager: the Owner-Operator. Raise: $300,000 via Reg D 506(b); minimum investment $25,000.
  • Preferred return: 8% per annum, cumulative, compounding quarterly, on unreturned capital until paid.
  • Distribution basis: Distributable Cash = EBITDA − maintenance capex − debt service − reserves (minimum operating cash floor $15,000 + accrued tax reserve) — not "net income," not "EBITDA" (Appendix G item 4).
  • Distributions: quarterly in arrears, 100% of Distributable Cash up to the preferred, then 60% investors / 40% operator; no catch-up. Mandatory annual tax distributions (Appendix G items 5–6).
  • Exit / liquidity: operator buyout right from the end of Year 5 at 4.0× trailing-twelve-month EBITDA (floor: invested capital + accrued preferred), otherwise a mandatory marketed sale in Year 6; drag-along + tag-along; transaction costs capped at 3% (Section 9.15).
  • Illustrative Year-5 value: 4.0 × $195,000 = $780,000 (3.5× floor = $682,500). Investor proceeds ≈ $588,000 at 4.0× — $300,000 preferred principal returned + 60% of ($780,000 − $300,000) — plus prior distributions (Section 9.15).

Competitive Advantages (Sections 2.1 / 4.1 / 13)

  • Proven model — 3 existing Twin Cities locations, 12+ years, $1.37M combined annual net sales
  • No verified direct competition in Woodbury — no African-Mediterranean fusion concept
  • Catering engine — 25.7% of sales at existing locations; Woodbury models 15–20% → 25% by Year 3
  • Woodbury demographics — ~75,000 residents (+12% since 2020), $117K median household income, 30–40% daytime surge
  • Delivery — 17.1% of sales, matched to existing locations
  • All-halal positioning — trust signal with minimal competition
