## 📈 2. Scaling Triggers (When to Scale)

### **DON'T Scale Yet If:**

❌ Pilot not producing consistently (3+ months)
❌ Mortality rate >20% in larvae
❌ Can't sell current production easily
❌ Unit economics unclear (cost/kg not tracked)
❌ No documented SOPs
❌ Cash flow negative
❌ Still learning basics

### **READY to Scale If:**

✅ 6+ months consistent production
✅ Larvae survival rate >80%
✅ Production cost <GHS 2.50/kg
✅ Selling price GHS 3.50-5.00/kg (50%+ margin)
✅ Demand exceeds current capacity
✅ SOPs documented and followed
✅ Positive cash flow 3+ months
✅ Working capital secured (1.5x scale-up cost)

## 🔄 4. Scaling Models

### **Model A: Organic Growth (Recommended)**

**Approach:** Reinvest profits, grow slowly, maintain control

**Pros:**
- ✅ Full ownership/control
- ✅ No debt pressure
- ✅ Learn as you grow
- ✅ Sustainable

**Cons:**
- ❌ Slower growth
- ❌ May miss market opportunities
- ❌ Capital constrained

**Timeline:** 4-6 years to Phase 3

---

### **Model B: Debt-Financed Growth**

**Approach:** Bank loan to accelerate expansion

**Pros:**
- ✅ Faster growth
- ✅ Capture market share early
- ✅ Keep equity

**Cons:**
- ❌ Debt service pressure
- ❌ Risk if things go wrong
- ❌ Collateral required

**Timeline:** 2-4 years to Phase 3

**Financing Options:**
| Source | Amount | Interest | Term |
|--------|--------|----------|------|
| ADB (Agricultural Development Bank) | GHS 100-500K | 18-24% | 3-5 years |
| GCB Bank | GHS 50-300K | 20-26% | 2-4 years |
| MASLOC | GHS 10-100K | 12-18% | 2-3 years |
| Youth in Agriculture | GHS 20-200K | Subsidized | 3-5 years |

---

### **Model C: Equity Investment**

**Approach:** Sell equity for capital + expertise

**Pros:**
- ✅ Significant capital
- ✅ Investor expertise/connections
- ✅ No debt service

**Cons:**
- ❌ Dilute ownership
- ❌ Investor expectations/pressure
- ❌ Exit timeline pressure

**Timeline:** 2-3 years to Phase 3

**Investor Types:**
- Impact investors (agriculture, sustainability)
- Angel investors (Ghana diaspora)
- Venture capital (agri-tech focused)
- Strategic (feed companies, integrators)

---

### **Model D: Franchise/Outgrower**

**Approach:** Train others to produce, you buy & sell

**Pros:**
- ✅ Rapid scale without capital
- ✅ Distributed production (lower risk)
- ✅ Social impact (jobs)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Quality control challenges
- ❌ Coordination complexity
- ❌ Lower margins

**Timeline:** 3-5 years to significant scale

**Structure:**
```
You (Central Hub)
│
├─ Outgrower 1 (100 m²) → You buy at GHS 2.50/kg
├─ Outgrower 2 (100 m²) → You buy at GHS 2.50/kg
├─ Outgrower 3 (100 m²) → You buy at GHS 2.50/kg
│
You process, package, sell at GHS 4.00-5.00/kg
Margin: GHS 1.00-2.00/kg (vs. GHS 1.50-2.50/kg if producing)
```

|------|------------|--------|------------|

| **Customer Concentration** | Medium | High | No customer >20% of revenue |
| **Regulatory Changes** | Low-Medium | Medium | Stay informed, comply, engage MoFA |
