SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Proven model (3 locations, 12+ years)
  • 25.7% catering revenue — proven institutional model
  • Unique cuisine = zero direct competition
  • All-halal = growing demand driver
  • 17.1% 3rd-party delivery (DoorDash, Uber, Grubhub)

Opportunities

  • Woodbury 12% growth = rising dining demand
  • No African-Med fusion competitor
  • Catering already proven at 25.7% — bigger market
  • Dinner only 16.1% of existing sales — huge runway
  • Second location or Minnetonka in Yr 3–5

Weaknesses

  • First suburban expansion — new territory
  • Limited Woodbury brand awareness vs nationals
  • Royalty + marketing (8%) compresses margins
  • No drive-thru (vs Panera, Chipotle)
  • Owner-operator learning curve

Threats

  • Economic downturn — rising rates could cut discretionary spend
  • Halal protein inflation — chicken, beef prices volatile
  • Labor market pressure — wage inflation in Twin Cities metro
  • Potential Cava entry — national chain could enter Woodbury
  • Lease escalation — 20–40% increase at 5–10 year renewal
  • Supply chain — specialty African ingredient disruption

Sensitivity Analysis

Two scenarios: Base case and a stress case (−20% covers, 32% COGS). The business stays cash-positive in both.

MetricBase CaseStress (−20% Covers, 32% COGS)
Year 1 Revenue$637,650$511,680

Return on $300K31.2%12.0%

Base Case

Conservative ramp (50→185 covers). EBITDA break-even Month 2, cumulative cash positive Month 3. Year-end net cash flow of $93,478 — a 31.2% return on $300K. The most likely scenario given existing brand recognition, catering model, and competitive gap.

Stress Case (−20% Covers, 32% COGS)

Revenue falls to ~$511,680 and COGS rises to 32% — EBITDA still lands at $36,090 (12.0% return), and the business stays cash-positive all year. Mitigation: cut labor to ~27%, tighten COGS to 27%, defer non-essential spend, accelerate catering.

Resilience: The $22K working capital reserve covers the base-case peak trough (−$1,965) more than 10× over, and the stress case never dips below zero. With no debt service, there is no interest burden or covenant risk to amplify a downturn.
