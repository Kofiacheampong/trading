1. Market Analysis

1.1 Why Woodbury? — Market Selection Rationale

Three Twin Cities suburbs were evaluated as primary candidates for the first Afro Deli franchise location outside the downtown/university core: Woodbury, Minnetonka, and Edina. Woodbury emerged as the clear winner.

1. Population & Growth (Critical Mass)

Woodbury is the largest of the three at ~75,000 residents and is growing at 12% since 2020 — three times faster than Minnetonka (+4%) and four times faster than Edina (+3%). This growth means new households are actively forming dining habits, and a new concept entering now captures the "shaping" of those habits rather than fighting entrenched competition.

2. Affluence Without Saturation

Median household income in Woodbury is $117,000 — nearly identical to Edina ($120,000) and well above Minnetonka ($105,000). However, Woodbury's restaurant density per capita is significantly lower. Edina has 186+ restaurants for 54,000 residents. Woodbury has roughly 140 restaurants for 75,000 residents. More people, fewer options — a structural advantage.

3. The Daytime Surge Advantage

Woodbury draws a 30–40% daytime population surge from Woodwinds Health Campus (major medical employer), I-94 corporate corridor (3M, Ecolab, State Farm regional offices), and regional retail power centers. This surge drives lunch traffic AND catering demand — both validated by existing Afro Deli locations.

4. Catering Infrastructure is Pre-Built

Afro Deli's existing locations generate 25.7% of revenue from catering — a figure Woodbury should match or exceed given its concentration of medical campuses, corporate offices, and schools. Woodbury's institutional base is larger and more corporate-heavy than what the St. Paul and Minneapolis locations serve.

5. Zero Direct Competition

Woodbury has Naf Naf Grill, Chipotle, Panera, Noodles & Co., and Crisp & Green. None offer African-Mediterranean fusion cuisine, halal-first positioning, cultural storytelling, or sambusas/Chicken Fantastic/Somali rice bowls. This is a genuine gap in the market.

6. Real Estate Economics

Woodbury lease rates ($20–$25/sq ft NNN) are 10–30% below Minnetonka and 20–40% below Edina. Combined with favorable permitting timelines (4–6 weeks vs. 6–12 weeks), Woodbury offers the lowest entry cost and fastest path to opening.

7. Expansion Sequencing Logic

Woodbury in Year 1 → Minnetonka in Years 3–5 → Edina express format in Year 5+. This sequencing builds from the largest market with the lowest barriers to the most expensive market last.
