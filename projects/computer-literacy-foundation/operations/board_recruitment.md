## 2. BOARD MEMBER ROLE DESCRIPTION

### Position: Board of Directors
**Organization:** [Foundation Name]  
**Term:** 3 years (renewable once)  
**Time Commitment:** 8-12 hours/month  
**Location:** [City/Region], Ghana (quarterly in-person meetings, monthly virtual)

### Role Overview

The Board of Directors provides strategic governance, oversight, and support to [Foundation Name], a non-profit organization dedicated to bringing computer literacy and digital skills training to rural Ghanaian communities.

### Key Responsibilities

**Governance & Strategy:**
- Attend quarterly board meetings (minimum 75% attendance required)
- Participate in strategic planning and annual goal-setting
- Approve annual budgets and monitor financial performance
- Ensure legal and regulatory compliance
- Uphold the organization's mission and values

**Fiduciary Oversight:**
- Review and approve annual budgets
- Monitor financial controls and audits
- Ensure responsible use of funds
- Approve major financial decisions

**Fundraising & Resource Mobilization:**
- Contribute personally (ability and willingness to donate)
- Make introductions to potential donors and partners
- Participate in fundraising events
- Leverage personal and professional networks

**Expertise & Guidance:**
- Provide subject matter expertise in your area
- Advise executive director on key decisions
- Serve on board committees as assigned
- Represent the organization positively in the community

**Oversight:**
- Hire, evaluate, and support the Executive Director
- Monitor program effectiveness and impact
- Ensure risk management and mitigation
- Approve major policies and decisions

### Time Commitment

| Activity | Frequency | Time |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Board meetings | Quarterly (in-person) | 4 hours each |
| Board meetings | Monthly (virtual) | 1.5 hours each |
| Committee meetings | Monthly/Bi-monthly | 1-2 hours each |
| Preparation (reading materials) | Monthly | 2-3 hours |
| Events/fundraising | Quarterly | 2-4 hours each |
| **Total per month** | | **8-12 hours** |

### Qualifications

**Required:**
- Commitment to the mission of digital literacy and rural development
- Integrity and strong ethical standards
- Willingness to contribute time and expertise
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team
- Strategic thinking and problem-solving skills

**Preferred (at least one per area):**
- Education/training program experience
- Technology/IT sector background
- Finance/accounting expertise (CPA, ACCA, or equivalent)
- Legal knowledge (non-profit law preferred)
- Community development experience
- Business/entrepreneurship background
- Fundraising experience
- Board experience (non-profit preferred)

### What We Offer

- Opportunity to create meaningful social impact
- Work with passionate, mission-driven team
- Professional development and networking
- Satisfaction of empowering rural Ghanaian youth
- Flexible participation (virtual options available)
- Annual board retreat and team building

### Phone Script: Follow-Up Call

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name]. I sent you an email last week about joining the board of a new digital literacy foundation I'm starting. Did you have a chance to see it?"

**If Yes:**
"Great! I'd love to schedule 30 minutes to tell you more about what we're doing and hear your thoughts. Would [suggest 2-3 times] work for you?"

**If No:**
"No problem at all - I know how busy things can get. Let me give you a quick overview now, and I can send you more information afterward."

**Elevator Pitch (30 seconds):**
"We're starting a foundation to bring computer training to rural communities in Ghana. 78% of rural Ghanaians have never used a computer, which limits their job opportunities. We're setting up learning centers with computers and trainers to teach digital skills. I'm building a founding board of 6-7 people with diverse expertise, and I immediately thought of you because of your [expertise]. Would you be interested in learning more?"

**If Interested:**
Schedule meeting and send follow-up email with materials.

**If Hesitant:**
"I completely understand. Would it be okay if I kept you updated on our progress? And if you know anyone else who might be interested, I'd appreciate an introduction."

**If Not Interested:**
"No problem at all, I appreciate your time. Would you be open to me reaching out in the future if we have specific questions in your area of expertise?"

---

### Meeting Agenda: First One-on-One

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

1. **Welcome & Personal Connection** (5 min)
   - Thank them for their time
   - Personal catch-up if you know them

2. **Foundation Overview** (10 min)
   - Mission and vision
   - The problem we're solving
   - Our approach and programs
   - Current status and timeline

3. **Board Role Discussion** (10 min)
   - Board responsibilities
   - Time commitment
   - What we're looking for in board members
   - Why we think they'd be a great fit

4. **Their Questions & Concerns** (10 min)
   - Answer questions
   - Address concerns
   - Discuss any hesitations

5. **Next Steps** (5 min)
   - If interested: Send constitution, schedule follow-up
   - If unsure: Give them time, send materials, follow up in 1 week
   - If not interested: Thank them, ask for referrals

**Materials to Bring/Share:**
- One-page foundation overview
- Board role description
- Draft constitution
- Preliminary budget and timeline
- Your bio and background
