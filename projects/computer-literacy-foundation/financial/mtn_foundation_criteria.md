## OVERVIEW

The MTN Foundation is the corporate social investment arm of MTN Ghana, focused on creating sustainable social impact in communities where MTN operates.

**Foundation Focus Areas:**
1. **Education** (Primary focus)
2. **Health**
3. **Economic Empowerment**
4. **Digital Inclusion** ← **Our Fit**

---

## ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

### ✅ Who Can Apply

**Eligible Organizations:**
- Registered non-profit organizations (NGOs)
- Companies Limited by Guarantee (non-profit)
- Trusts with charitable status
- Community-based organizations (CBOs)
- Faith-based organizations (for community programs)
- Educational institutions
- Government partnerships (with clear implementation partner)

**Registration Requirements:**
- Must be registered in Ghana
- Minimum 2 years of operation (preferred, not always required)
- Valid registration certificate from Registrar General
- Tax exemption certificate (if applicable)
- Good standing with regulatory bodies

### ❌ Who Cannot Apply

- For-profit companies
- Political organizations
- Religious organizations (for religious activities)
- Individuals
- Organizations with history of fraud or mismanagement

| Criteria | Points | What They Look For |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Alignment with MTN Foundation Focus** | 20 pts | Clear fit with education/digital inclusion |

**They Prioritize Projects That:**
- ✅ Benefit underserved/rural communities

## APPLICATION PROCESS

### Step-by-Step

**Step 1: Initial Inquiry (Week 1)**
- Contact MTN Foundation office
- Request application guidelines
- Submit brief concept note (2-3 pages)
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for response

**Step 2: Concept Note Review (Week 2-4)**
- Foundation reviews concept
- May request meeting/presentation
- Invitation to submit full proposal (if interested)
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Step 3: Full Proposal Submission (Week 5-8)**
- Submit complete application package
- Include all required documents
- **Timeline:** You get 2-4 weeks to prepare

**Step 4: Due Diligence (Week 9-14)**
- Site visit by Foundation staff
- Reference checks
- Financial review
- **Timeline:** 4-6 weeks

**Step 5: Approval Committee (Week 15-18)**
- Board/Committee review
- Decision made
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Step 6: Grant Agreement (Week 19-22)**
- Negotiate terms
- Sign agreement
- First disbursement
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Total Timeline:** 5-6 months from inquiry to first disbursement

## APPLICATION TIPS

### Do's ✅

1. **Align with MTN's priorities** - Emphasize digital inclusion, education, youth
2. **Show MTN integration** - Mention MTN connectivity, Mobile Money, etc.
3. **Highlight visibility** - Promise prominent MTN branding
4. **Be specific** - Clear numbers, timelines, outcomes
5. **Show community support** - Letters from chiefs, assemblies, schools
6. **Demonstrate sustainability** - How will it continue after grant?
7. **Include M&E** - How will you measure and report impact?
8. **Budget realistically** - Don't overinflate, show value for money
9. **Follow up** - Professional follow-up every 2-3 weeks
10. **Be patient** - Process takes 5-6 months

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't submit generic proposal** - Tailor to MTN Foundation specifically
2. **Don't overpromise** - Be realistic about what you can achieve
3. **Don't ignore guidelines** - Follow their format exactly
4. **Don't skip M&E** - They want to see measurable impact
5. **Don't hide challenges** - Acknowledge risks and mitigation
6. **Don't forget branding** - MTN wants visibility for their investment
7. **Don't disappear after submission** - Stay in touch professionally
8. **Don't submit incomplete application** - Check all requirements
