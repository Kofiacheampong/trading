## PROBLEM STATEMENT

### Digital Exclusion in Rural Ghana

Despite MTN's excellent network coverage, many rural Ghanaians lack the skills to leverage digital tools for economic advancement:

- 78% of rural residents have never used a computer
- Only 15% can confidently use internet services
- Mobile phone usage is high, but digital literacy is low
- Youth unemployment remains at 28% in rural areas
- Women face additional barriers to technology access

### Community Need

[Community Name] and surrounding areas (population: ~15,000) have:
- No computer training facilities within 25km
- High youth population (45% under age 25)
- Strong mobile phone penetration (MTN network)
- Employers citing digital skills as hiring barrier
- Youth eager to learn but no access to training

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Goal

Establish the "MTN Digital Skills Hub" to provide accessible digital literacy training to 500 rural community members, with focus on youth and women.

### Objectives

1. Set up fully-equipped computer lab with 20 workstations
2. Train 500 individuals in 12 months
3. Achieve 60% female participation
4. Partner with MTN for connectivity and volunteer support
5. Create pathway to employment for graduates

### Program Components

#### Component 1: MTN Digital Skills Hub Setup

**Facility:**
- 100 m² training center in [Community Name]
- 20 desktop computers
- 1 projector for demonstrations
- Reliable MTN fiber/4G internet connectivity
- Backup power system
- Branded as "MTN Digital Skills Hub"

#### Component 2: Training Programs

**Course Offerings:**

| Course | Duration | Students | Focus |
