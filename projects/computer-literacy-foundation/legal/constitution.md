## ARTICLE 3: OBJECTS

The objects for which the Foundation is established are:

### 3.1 Primary Objects

(a) To promote digital literacy and computer education in rural and underserved communities in Ghana

(b) To provide accessible, affordable, and quality technology training to individuals regardless of their economic status, age, gender, or physical ability

(c) To bridge the digital divide between urban and rural communities in Ghana

(d) To empower individuals with digital skills for education, employment, and economic advancement

### 3.2 Secondary Objects

(e) To establish and operate computer learning centers in rural communities

(f) To develop and deliver curriculum in computer literacy, productivity software, digital skills, and specialized technology tracks

(g) To partner with schools, communities, government agencies, and private sector organizations to expand digital literacy access

(h) To provide scholarships and financial assistance to underprivileged individuals seeking digital skills training

(i) To conduct research on digital literacy needs and effective training methodologies in rural Ghana

(j) To advocate for policies that promote digital inclusion and equitable access to technology

(k) To collaborate with international organizations, donors, and development agencies on digital literacy initiatives

(l) To engage in any other lawful activities that further the above objects

### 3.3 Non-Profit Nature

(m) The income and property of the Foundation shall be applied solely towards the promotion of its objects

(n) No portion thereof shall be paid or transferred directly or indirectly to the members of the Foundation except as permitted under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992)

---

## ARTICLE 4: POWERS

To achieve its objects, the Foundation shall have the following powers:

(a) To acquire, hold, and dispose of land, buildings, and other property

(b) To enter into contracts, agreements, and partnerships

(c) To receive grants, donations, and contributions from individuals, organizations, and governments

(d) To charge fees for services rendered, subject to the non-profit nature of the Foundation

(e) To employ staff and engage consultants as necessary

(f) To open and operate bank accounts

(g) To invest funds in accordance with the Companies Act and prudent investment practices

(h) To do all such other lawful things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects

---

## ARTICLE 5: MEMBERSHIP

### 5.1 Categories of Membership

The Foundation shall have the following categories of members:

(a) **Founding Members:** The original subscribers to the Constitution

(b) **Ordinary Members:** Individuals admitted by the Board who support the Foundation's objects

(c) **Corporate Members:** Organizations that support the Foundation's objects

(d) **Honorary Members:** Individuals conferred honorary membership by the Board for distinguished service

### 5.2 Membership Criteria

Membership shall be open to any person or organization that:

(a) Supports the objects of the Foundation

(b) Agrees to abide by this Constitution

(c) Pays any prescribed membership fees (if applicable)

(d) Is approved by the Board of Directors

### 5.3 Rights of Members

Members shall have the right to:

(a) Attend and vote at General Meetings

(b) Elect and be elected to the Board of Directors

(c) Access information about the Foundation's activities (subject to confidentiality)

(d) Participate in Foundation programs and events

### 5.4 Cessation of Membership

Membership shall cease upon:

(a) Death (for individual members)

(b) Dissolution (for corporate members)

(c) Resignation by written notice to the Secretary

(d) Expulsion by the Board for conduct detrimental to the Foundation

## ARTICLE 6: BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### 6.1 Composition

The Foundation shall be governed by a Board of Directors consisting of:

(a) A minimum of 5 (five) Directors

(b) A maximum of 9 (nine) Directors

(c) At least 2 (two) Directors must be ordinarily resident in Ghana

### 6.2 First Directors

The first Directors of the Foundation shall be:

1. **[Name]** - [Position, e.g., Founder/Executive Director]
2. **[Name]** - [Position, e.g., Chairperson]
3. **[Name]** - [Position, e.g., Secretary]
4. **[Name]** - [Position, e.g., Treasurer]
5. **[Name]** - [Position]
6. **[Name]** - [Position]

### 6.3 Election and Term

(a) Directors shall be elected by members at the Annual General Meeting

(b) Each Director shall serve a term of 3 (three) years

(c) Directors may serve a maximum of 2 (two) consecutive terms

(d) Casual vacancies shall be filled by the Board until the next AGM

### 6.4 Roles and Responsibilities

**Chairperson:**
- Preside at Board and General Meetings
- Provide strategic leadership
- Represent the Foundation externally
- Ensure Board effectiveness

**Secretary:**
- Maintain records and registers
- Prepare meeting notices and minutes
- Handle correspondence
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements

**Treasurer:**
- Oversee financial management
- Present financial reports
- Ensure proper financial controls
- Liaise with auditors

**Other Directors:**
- Participate in Board decisions
- Serve on committees as assigned
- Provide expertise and guidance
- Represent the Foundation as delegated

### 6.5 Board Meetings

(a) The Board shall meet at least 4 (four) times per year (quarterly)

(b) Special meetings may be called by the Chairperson or upon request by 3 Directors

(c) Notice of at least 7 (seven) days shall be given for all meetings

(d) Quorum shall be 50% of Directors or 3 Directors, whichever is greater

(e) Decisions shall be by simple majority; Chairperson has casting vote in case of tie

(f) Meetings may be held in person or via electronic means

### 6.6 Board Committees

The Board may establish committees including:

(a) **Finance and Audit Committee** - Oversee financial matters

(b) **Program Committee** - Oversee program development and implementation

(c) **Fundraising and Partnerships Committee** - Lead resource mobilization

(d) **Governance and Nominations Committee** - Handle governance and board recruitment

---

## ARTICLE 7: MANAGEMENT

### 7.1 Executive Director

(a) The Foundation shall have an Executive Director (ED) who shall be the Chief Executive Officer

(b) The ED shall be appointed by the Board and may be a member of the Board

(c) The ED shall be responsible for day-to-day management of the Foundation

(d) The ED shall report to the Board and implement Board decisions

### 7.2 Duties of Executive Director

(a) Implement Foundation programs and activities

(b) Prepare annual budgets and work plans

(c) Recruit, supervise, and manage staff

(d) Represent the Foundation in dealings with external parties (as delegated)

(e) Prepare reports for the Board

(f) Ensure compliance with laws and regulations

(g) Perform other duties as assigned by the Board

### 7.3 Staff

(a) The Foundation may employ such staff as necessary for its operations

(b) Staff shall be appointed based on merit and qualifications

(c) Staff terms and conditions shall be determined by the Board

---

## ARTICLE 8: FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

### 8.1 Financial Year

The financial year of the Foundation shall end on 31st December of each year.

### 8.2 Funds

The funds of the Foundation shall be derived from:

(a) Grants and donations

(b) Membership fees (if applicable)

(c) Income from programs and services

(d) Investment income

(e) Fundraising activities

(f) Any other lawful sources

### 8.3 Bank Accounts

(a) The Foundation shall open and maintain bank accounts with reputable financial institutions in Ghana

(b) Accounts shall be operated by signatories appointed by the Board

(c) Minimum of 2 (two) signatories required for all transactions

(d) Signatories shall include at least one Director and the Executive Director

### 8.4 Financial Controls

(a) Proper books of accounts shall be kept

(b) Internal financial controls shall be implemented

(c) Expenditure shall be authorized in accordance with Board-approved limits

(d) Assets shall be properly safeguarded and insured

### 8.5 Audit

(a) The Foundation shall appoint an independent external auditor annually

(b) Financial statements shall be audited annually

(c) Audited financial statements shall be presented to the AGM

(d) Auditor shall be rotated every 5 (five) years

---

## ARTICLE 9: GENERAL MEETINGS

### 9.1 Annual General Meeting (AGM)

(a) An AGM shall be held once every calendar year

(b) Not more than 15 (fifteen) months shall elapse between consecutive AGMs

(c) Notice of at least 21 (twenty-one) days shall be given

(d) AGM business shall include:
   - Receiving reports from the Board
   - Approving audited financial statements
   - Electing Directors (where applicable)
   - Appointing auditors
   - Any other business

### 9.2 Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM)

(a) An EGM may be called by the Board at any time

(b) An EGM shall be called upon written request by members representing at least 10% of voting membership

(c) Notice of at least 14 (fourteen) days shall be given

(d) EGM shall address only the business specified in the notice

### 9.3 Quorum and Voting

(a) Quorum at General Meetings shall be 25% of members or 10 members, whichever is less

(b) Each member present shall have one vote

(c) Decisions shall be by simple majority

(d) Chairperson has casting vote in case of tie

(e) Proxy voting shall be permitted with written authorization

---

## ARTICLE 10: AMENDMENTS TO CONSTITUTION

(a) This Constitution may be amended by a special resolution passed at a General Meeting

(b) Notice of proposed amendment shall be included in the meeting notice

(c) Amendment shall require approval by at least 75% of members present and voting

(d) Amendments shall be filed with the Registrar General's Department

---

## ARTICLE 11: DISSOLUTION

(a) The Foundation may be dissolved by a special resolution passed at a General Meeting

(b) Notice of proposed dissolution shall be given at least 30 (thirty) days before the meeting

(c) Resolution shall require approval by at least 75% of members

(d) Upon dissolution, after payment of all debts and liabilities, remaining assets shall be transferred to another non-profit organization with similar objects, as determined by the members

(e) No assets shall be distributed to members

(f) Dissolution shall be filed with the Registrar General's Department and relevant authorities

---

## ARTICLE 12: INDEMNIFICATION

Every Director, officer, and employee of the Foundation shall be indemnified by the Foundation against all losses and liabilities incurred in the performance of their duties, provided such person acted honestly, in good faith, and in the best interests of the Foundation.

---

## ARTICLE 13: CONFLICT OF INTEREST

(a) Directors and officers shall disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest

(b) Interested persons shall not participate in discussions or voting on matters where they have a conflict

(c) The Foundation shall maintain a Conflict of Interest Register

(d) Transactions with interested parties shall be on arm's length terms and approved by disinterested Directors

---

## ARTICLE 14: GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 14.1 Governing Law

This Constitution shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Ghana, particularly the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992).

### 14.2 Seal

The Foundation shall have a common seal which shall be kept in the custody of the Secretary. The seal shall only be affixed to documents authorized by the Board and signed by two authorized signatories.

### 14.3 Notices

Notices to members shall be delivered by hand, post, email, or other electronic means to the address registered with the Foundation.

### 14.4 Language

The official language of the Foundation shall be English.
