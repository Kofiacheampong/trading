# 🎬 Faceless Finance Channel — Plan v1

**Concept:** Small-account trader, real systems, real data. No face, no hype, no courses.
Voiceover + charts + screen recordings of actual experiments. The content IS the process.

**Why this works (2026):** YouTube rejects "inauthentic content" (AI voice + stock footage +
recycled clips). Original data + original results = content they can't flag, in the highest-RPM
niche (finance $10-30 RPM).

**Format:** 8-12 min long-form, 1-2 uploads/week. Title = the number. Thumbnail = the contrast.

**Content engine (all from Kofi's real systems):**
1. Sim experiments (dip-buys, put credits, call flags, OR-fade) → "I paper-traded X for N days"
2. Weekly Market Pulse → weekly market breakdown
3. Small-account options journey (real P&L, $400 account, spreads only)
4. The gate system → "I don't trust my strategy until the data says so"
5. Future: EPL betting agent CLV journey (transparent, data-first betting content)

**Cadence:** Episode 1 = the OR-fade backtest (biggest, most complete story).
Then: sim weekly check-ins + one experiment deep-dive per month.

**Tools ($0-50/mo):** CapCut (edit), Canva (thumbnails), OBS (screen recordings),
ElevenLabs free tier or Kofi's own voice for VO. No stock footage needed.

**Monetization path:** YPP (500 subs/3k hrs early tier → 1k subs/4k hrs for ads),
then affiliate (brokerages, data tools), then channel is an asset.
Realistic: $0 for 3-6 months, $200-1,200/mo at 1-5k subs if retention holds.
