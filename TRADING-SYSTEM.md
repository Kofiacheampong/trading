# 🎯 The Weekly $400 Options System

A repeatable system for trading options with a small account.

---

## Core Rules (Don't Break These)

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| 🟢 **$100-150 max risk per trade** | So 1 loss doesn't kill the account |
| 🟢 **1-2 trades per week max** | Quality over quantity |
| 🟢 **Always defined risk** (credit/debit spreads only) | Never naked options |
| 🟢 **7-14 DTE** (days to expiration) | Enough time to be right, not so much theta kills you |
| 🟢 **Close at 50% max profit; HARD stop at 50% max loss** | Take profits, cut theta decay before it eats the full debit |
| 🟢 **No holding over the weekend** | Too much gap risk for small accounts |
| 🟢 **Trade what you know** (HIMS, FCX, SPY, NVDA) | Stick to 3-5 tickers, learn them deep |

## The 3 Types of Weekly Trades

### Type A: Bull Call Spread (Buy Call, Sell Higher Call)
**What happened today.**
- Buy an ITM call → Sell a further OTM call
- You pay a debit, profit if stock goes up
- Trade this when: **Stock has positive catalyst, momentum is up**

### Type B: Bear Put Spread (Buy Put, Sell Lower Put)
- Buy an ITM put → Sell a further OTM put
- You pay a debit, profit if stock goes down
- Trade this when: **Stock is overbought, earnings miss, sector down**

### Type C: Put Credit Spread (Sell Put, Buy Lower Put) → BULLISH
**Your next trade should be this.**
- You COLLECT a credit instead of paying
- If stock stays above your short strike, you keep the money
- Higher win rate (70%+) but smaller profits per trade (~$20-40)
- Trade this when: **Stock is at support, IV is high, you want high probability**

## Weekly Process (Every Monday)

### Step 1: Scan (10 min)
Look at your watchlist. Check these:
1. **Price action** — is it up or down in the last 5 days?
2. **News/catalysts** — any upgrades, earnings coming?
3. **IV rank** — is IV above 50%? (Higher IV = better premiums)
4. **Support/resistance** — obvious levels?

### Step 2: Pick a Direction
| Signal | Trade |
|--------|-------|
| Stock at support, analyst upgrade | **Type A** (bull call spread) or **Type C** (put credit spread) |
| Stock at resistance, overbought RSI | **Type B** (bear put spread) |
| Stock in the middle, no clear direction | **Type C** (put credit spread) — highest probability |
| IV is high (>70%) | **Type C** (sell premium, collect fat premiums) |

### Step 3: Size It
| Account Size | Risk Per Trade | Trades Per Week |
|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| $400 | $100-150 | 1-2 |
| $500 | $100-150 | 1-2 |
| $750 | $150-200 | 1-2 |
| $1000 | $150-250 | 1-2 |

**Never use more than 50% of account on one trade.** Period.

### Step 4: Enter
- Use **limit orders**, never market orders
- For debit spreads: enter at the **mid price** or better
- For credit spreads: enter at the **mid price** or better
- Don't chase — if the price moves away, wait or pass

### Step 5: Exit
| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| +50% of max profit | **Close the trade** — take the win |
| +100% max profit | **Close immediately** — max achieved |
| -50% of max loss | **HARD STOP — close it.** Debit spreads decay fast; don't ride it to -100% |
| -100% max loss | Should never happen if you honor the -50% stop. Accept it, move on |
| Credit spread (Type C) | Stop = cost reaches **2x collected credit** (same as fade sim) |
| 1 day left to expiry and not winning | Close for whatever you can get |

## Recommended Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Sunday** | Review open positions. Scan watchlist. Make note of any earnings/events this week |
| **Monday** | Open 1 trade (morning, after first 30 min of market settling) |
| **Tuesday-Thu** | Manage the trade. Check once per day. Set alerts at breakeven and max profit |
| **Friday** | Close any remaining positions by 2:30 PM (don't hold into close) |

## The Golden Rules

1. ✅ **You will lose trades.** Accept it. 40-50% loss rate is normal for debit spreads.
2. ✅ **It's better to miss a trade than force one.** No setup this week? Cool. Don't trade.
3. ✅ **Size down if you're on a losing streak.** Lost 2 in a row? Trade $50 risk until you feel right.
4. ❌ **No revenge trading.** Lost $100? Don't bet the remaining $300 on one trade to "get it back."
5. ❌ **No weeklies expiring that Friday.** Minimum 7 DTE.
6. ❌ **No earnings plays until you have 6 months of experience.**

## How to Review Your Week (Every Saturday)

Answer these 3 questions:
1. Did I follow my rules? (Yes/No — if no, fix it before next week)
2. What was the setup? (Price action, catalyst, IV)
3. What would I do differently? (Entry timing? Strike selection? Size?)

Log it. One sentence. After 10 weeks you'll see patterns in what works for you.
