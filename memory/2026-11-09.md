## Garnsh v2.1 — financial surgery on the founders' plan (base case as headline)

Kofi chose **base case as the plan, conservative as the survival slide**. Rebuilt v2's financials from scratch rather than patching.

**Built `projects/garnsh-v2/model.py`** — 36-month monthly cash model, single source of truth. Outputs `model.json`; plan tables and deck figures are copied from it, not hand-typed. Kept v2 pricing ($59 core + $40 boards + $1,500 setup; $99 ordering; $5,000+$149 app).

**The finding that matters:** the $20,000 is never fully spent. Peak drawdown is **$14,128 in May 2027** (trough cash $5,872); revenue exceeds costs from **June**; capital recovered **October**. The trough is caused by front-loaded spend (design/legal/insurance/brand land before the first invoice), not by the sales ramp — run the ramp 30% slower and the trough only falls to $5,410. Hence: design paid in three milestones, Toast cert moved to Q3, brand budget trimmed $2,500→$2,000.

**Numbers (base):** 2027 rev $58.1K / net +$23.0K / exit MRR $12.8K. 2028 rev $501K / net +$215K / ARR $753K. 2029 rev $1.33M / net +$503K / ARR $1.77M / cash $761K. ARPU build $61→$98→$118. Fully loaded GM 86.8% / 80.5% / 83.9% (support + onboarding in COGS; software-only is ~95%). LTV multi ~$5,830, CAC $1,200, payback 15.1 mo, 4.9x. Single-unit LTV ~$1,500, 5.1 mo, 10x.

**Conservative case now has its own cost line** (founders only, no hires): 2027 rev $18.5K / net +$103 / cash $20.1K; 2028 rev $126K / founders $30K each / net +$13.9K / cash floor **$7,841**; 2029 rev $449K / founders $60K each / net +$220K / cash $254K. Its floor is in 2028, not 2027, and the flex is founder pay.

**Corrections vs v2:** (1) revenue was inflated 4–6% by applying exit-year ARPU across the whole year → 2029 is $1.33M not $1.42M (exit ARR $1.77M is the better headline); (2) GM now includes support/onboarding labour; (3) setup fee $1,500 only breaks even at 2 h/location onboarding — at 3 h it loses $660/brand; raising to $2,500 adds $31K (2028) + $46K (2029); (4) "cash-positive by Sept" replaced with real gates (June revenue-positive, October capital recovered); (5) sensitivity: if Toast denies ordering **and** costs stay at base levels, 2029 is −$40K → fall back to conservative cost line.

**Deliverables:** `projects/garnsh-v2/garnsh-business-plan-v2.1.md` (source of truth), `.docx` (Zoho-editable, via md2docx.py), `.pdf` (19pp, WeasyPrint).
