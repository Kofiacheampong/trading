# Ghana Ginger — Feasibility Sketch
*Prepared Aug 3, 2026 · Status: sector scan + unit economics, pre-fieldwork*

---

## 1. Market State (2026)

**Ghana is in a ginger SHORTAGE — and it's disease-driven, not demand-driven.**
- April 2026: government confirmed a **ginger disease outbreak** (wilt/rot) driving a price surge; traders importing from **China and Nigeria** to fill gaps (ModernGhana, CitiNewsroom).
- Ginger was in the top-10 biggest price risers with a **~61% YoY price increase** (Apr 2026). A sack that sold for GHC 300-400 now goes for far more.
- Ghana is a **net importer** of ginger despite growing it locally — exports only ~$1.75M/yr (Mar 2025-Feb 2026), fresh-ginger exports ~$34K. Export price ~$535/ton (+77% YoY, 2024).
- Global market: ~$4.5B, ~7%/yr growth. The gap in Ghana is real and current.

**Why the shortage exists (the moat opportunity):**
1. **Disease** (bacterial wilt + fusarium) wipes out fields — most farmers use poor, infected seed stock
2. Fragmented smallholders, no organized supply chain
3. **No drying infrastructure** — sun-drying = mold/quality loss; processors want dried product
4. Result: processors pay **import prices** for something that grows in Ghanaian soil

## 2. Unit Economics (1-acre pilot, conservative)

**Inputs (2026 prices, GHC):**
| Item | Est. cost |
|---|---|
| Seed rhizomes (800-1,000 kg/acre @ GHC 15-25/kg in shortage) | GHC 12,000-25,000 |
| Land prep + planting + weeding + harvest labor | GHC 6,000-10,000 |
| Fertilizer (poultry manure — FREE synergy with existing farm) | GHC 1,000-3,000 |
| Disease control + miscellaneous | GHC 2,000-4,000 |
| **Total per acre** | **GHC 23,000-40,000 (~$1,400-2,500)** |

**Outputs:**
- Yield: 5-8 tons/acre fresh (Ghana avg ~5 tons/acre; 12.5+ tons/ha with good agronomy; disease cuts this — plan for the low end)
- Fresh farmgate: pre-shortage GHC 5/kg (250/50kg bag); **now elevated — likely GHC 10-20/kg farmgate in shortage zones**
- **Fresh revenue/acre: ~GHC 50,000-100,000 in a shortage year** (5,000 kg × 10-20)
- Net: **GHC 20,000-60,000/acre** depending on price + yield achieved

**The processing uplift (where the real margin is):**
- Dried ginger = ~20-25% of fresh weight (4-5 kg fresh → 1 kg dried)
- Dried/powder sells at 2-4x fresh-equivalent value and stores for months
- 1 acre → ~1,000-1,250 kg dried → GHC 80,000-100,000+ at powder/dried prices
- A simple drying shed + grinder (GHC 15,000-30,000 capex) converts commodity into product

## 3. Buyers (who actually buys)

**Local (fast cash, low friction):**
- Market traders — Kejetia (Kumasi), Makola/Agbogbloshie (Accra), Nima; regional Volta markets
- Sobolo/ginger-drink makers (big consumer: sobolo is national)
- Hotels/restaurants (farmstay synergy: "we grow our own ginger")
- Shito producers, food processors, pharma/tea makers

**Processors (volume, contract-friendly):**
- Asutifi Processing & Services Centre (dried ginger, chili, powder — active buyer)
- 39+ registered ginger exporters in Ghana; 56 overseas buyers importing from Ghana
- Export channels: freshdi.com, go4worldbusiness B2B leads; GEPA export support

## 4. The Strategy (phased, mirrors the poultry playbook)

**Phase 1 (Season 1):** 0.5-1 acre pilot. Clean/certified seed ONLY. Crop rotation + drainage + early disease scouting. Sell fresh to traders + sobolo makers to prove crop and recover cash. Target: 5+ tons/acre, no disease wipeout.

**Phase 2 (Season 2):** Add drying shed + grinder. Sell dried/powder to processors (Asutifi-type), hotels, pharma. Now you're selling a product, not a commodity — and storage kills the price-volatility risk.

**Phase 3 (optional):** Contract growing with outgrowers (your seed + disease protocol + guaranteed purchase) — scales without your own land. Fits the existing loan/grant pipeline (Mastercard/MTN, 1D1F agro-processing angle).

## 5. Risks (honest)

1. **Seed scarcity/price** — shortage inflates seed cost; poor seed = disease = the exact failure mode that created the shortage
2. **Disease wipeout** — the #1 killer; protocol discipline is the whole game
3. **Price normalization** — govt relief + production recovery will eventually cool the surge; the dried/processed layer hedges this
4. **Labor intensity** — planting/weeding/harvesting are labor-heavy; plan the workforce
5. **Cedi/export math** — export revenue in USD, costs in GHC; currency risk if going the export route
